Quick guide

How to play

  1. Press Start game to begin moving.
  2. Guide the snake with the arrow keys, WASD keys, swipe gestures, or the on-screen controls.
  3. Eat red fruit to grow and earn a point. Every 3 points increases the level, speed, and obstacles.
  4. Do not hit a wall, an obstacle, or your own tail. Try to beat your best score!
